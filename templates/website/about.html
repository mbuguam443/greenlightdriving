About Greenlight Driving School

Greenlight Defensive Driving School is a leading NTSA-certified institution dedicated to producing safe, confident, and responsible drivers. Established with a vision to reduce road accidents through quality driver education, we have trained thousands of students across Kenya.

Our team of certified instructors brings decades of combined experience, utilizing modern training vehicles and proven teaching methodologies. We believe that every driver deserves the best possible education to navigate Kenya's roads safely.

With multiple branches across the country, we make quality driving education accessible to everyone. From beginner courses to advanced defensive driving, Greenlight is your trusted partner on the road to becoming a skilled, responsible driver.
